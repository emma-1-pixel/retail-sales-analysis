# retail-sales-analysis
##Data Cleaning
Checked 9,994 rows: no missing values in Sales, Profit or Postal Code, no duplicate Row IDs, and consistent text in Region, Category and Segment.
## Key Findings
1.Tables, Bookcases and Supplies were unprofitable. Tables alone lost $17.7K (margin -8.6%) despite $207K in sales, while Copiers earned the highest profit ($55.6K, 37.2% margin).

2.West was the most profitable region ($108K profit, 14.9% margin). Central had the lowest margin (7.9%): it sold $501K but earned only $39.7K profit, less than South despite higher sales.

3. Discounts above 20% were unprofitable. Orders with 21-40% and 40%+ discounts lost $35.8K and $99.6K respectively, a combined loss of about $135K, while orders with no discount earned $321K profit.

4.Sales grew 51% from 2014 ($484K) to 2017 ($733K), and profit grew 89% ($49.5K to $93.4K). Profit margin rose from 10.2% to 13.4% by 2016, then dipped to 12.7% in 2017.
